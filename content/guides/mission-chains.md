> This guide names planets and stations as well as missions. If you are reading
> it mid-playthrough, that is the wider spoiler.

There are **69 objectives** in the game, and they are not a quest line. Fifty-
two of them have no prerequisite at all — you can attempt them the moment you
can physically get there. Only seventeen are gated behind something else.

That structure is worth understanding before you plan a route, because the game
presents its objectives as a list and leaves you to work out that most of the
list is already open.

## Three kinds, and they behave differently

**Package — 35 of them.** Go somewhere, find a crashed cargo unit, bring it
back. These are the game's supply line: nearly every material in the game
enters your inventory through a Package objective, and they are the ones worth
detouring for. They have a single start location and no destination, because
the destination is wherever you found it.

**Delivery — 14 of them.** Carry a named object from one station to another.
Every Delivery names both ends, and **every one requires you to be carrying a
specific component** to start. To The Moon needs an [[part:IceCreamFreezer]]; Trial By
Thrust needs a [[part:MediumElectricThruster]]; Rimcore Cryogen needs two Rimcore
Turbopumps. These are the objectives that fail before they begin, because you
arrived without the thing.

**Special — 20 of them.** Everything else: heat a crystal to 750,000°C, survey
a wind pattern, detonate something. These carry the story and most of the
chains.

## Nineteen missions want you to bring something

This is the most common way an objective turns out to be unavailable, and it is
worth reading the requirement before you fly rather than after. The full list
of objectives that need a component in hand:

- **To The Moon** — Ice Cream Freezer
- **Drop The Depth Diver** — Submarine
- **Unstable Delivery** — [[part:UnstableLightingCell]]
- **[[part:OverloadingCrystal]]** — Overloading Crystal
- **Trial By Thrust** — Medium Electric Thruster
- **Knock Knock** — Angry Box
- **Wind Test** — [[part:WindShieldGenerator]]
- **Night Vision** — [[part:NightVisionCamera]]
- **Baobara Interference** — [[part:BaobaraInterferencemeter]]
- **Tree Lid** — Baobara Interferencemeter _and_ a [[part:LargeGate]]
- **[[part:ClimaBomb]]** — Clima Bomb
- **[[part:CandyBouquet]]** — Candy Bouquet
- **Rock Obsession** — Bomb C4 and a Decoupler Quarter
- **Green Trench** — three Green Trench Pumps
- **Rimcore Cryogen** — two Rimcore Turbopumps
- **Crystal Twins** — [[part:Thermoscan]]
- **Lava Sucking** — two Lava Pumping Tanks
- **Damaged Thruster** — [[part:DamagedLargeFuelThruster]]
- **Tungsten Nanocube** — Tungsten Nanocube

Note the pattern in the last group: several of these require _multiples_ of an
expensive part. Green Trench wants three pumps at 770 mass and 90 P/s each.
That is a ship built for one objective.

## The chains

Seventeen objectives have a prerequisite. Two of them are gated on **reaching a
place**, not on finishing a mission — a distinction the game does not make
visible:

- The three **Basalt Candyflower** packages open when you find the **Candy
  Vein** station on Basalt.
- **Crystal Twins** opens when you find **Altimirror** on Rimshell.

The rest follow other objectives:

- **Baobara Interference** → **Tree Lid** → **What Happened To Baobab** — the
  longest single-planet chain, three deep, and the Interferencemeter you need
  for the first two is carried, not built.
- **Rock Obsession** → **Find Outcast**, on Basalt.
- **Crystal Twins** → **Project Altimirror**, the wormhole.
- **Clima Bomb** → **Mastodon Transport**.
- **Return Of The Herd** → **Pinktwin Reindeer Antlers**.
- **The three Candyflowers** → **Candy Bouquet**, which needs all three.
- **Aundara Plasma** → **Wind Test**.
- **Ashbelt Damaged Thruster** → **Damaged Thruster**.
- **Ascensia Brachiosaurus Egg** → **Triceratops Transport**.
- **Tungsten Nanocube** → **Twins Tunnel Gold** _and_ **Twins Tunnel
  Pink** → **The Long Way Home**, which needs both.

That last one is the deepest chain in the game and the only one that branches
and rejoins. It runs Goldtwin to Pinktwin and back, and everything before it is
optional.

## Ten objectives give you nothing

Not every objective pays. Ten have no reward at all in the files:

Basalt Candyflower A, B and C; Ashbelt Damaged Thruster; Baobara Interference;
Find Outcast; Helirion Lost Packages; Project Altimirror; Return Of The Herd;
The Long Way Home.

Look at the list and the logic is clear: most of them are **links in a chain**
rather than ends in themselves — the Candyflowers pay out through Candy
Bouquet, Baobara Interference pays out through Tree Lid. The other two, Project
Altimirror and The Long Way Home, are the ends of the game. Their reward is
that they are the ending.

## Four objectives are hidden

The game does not list them; you find them by finding them. They are **the
Drumkit on Earth** — on top of Headquarters — and **three lost packages on
Helirion**, which have their own umbrella objective, Helirion Lost Packages,
tying them together.

Helirion is the lightest-gravity planet in the game, which makes hunting around
on it unusually cheap. That is probably why three of the four hidden things are
there.

## Which missions open a material

This is the part worth planning around. A reward does not unlock one part — it
fills a **stock pool**, and every part made of that material becomes placeable
at once. [How parts unlock](/guides/how-parts-unlock) explains the mechanism;
what follows is where each pool comes from.

You start with frame, glass, cables, math blocks, basic controllers and a
little else. Everything below is missions only.

**Pipes — fuel plumbing.** Four sources: **Baobara First Pipes**, Helirion Lost
Package 0, CoronaSilva More Blue Fuel, Kovo Maneuvers. First Pipes is the one
that says so on the tin — its objective text calls it the package for "your
first fuel-powered spaceship" — and it brings the pipe meters, manual valve,
electric valve and a [[part:FuelAnalyzer]] with it. Fuel thrusters are three to five
times better per unit of mass than electric ones, so this is the single
biggest capability jump in the game.

**Nanopipes — one source in the entire game.** **The Giant Toothpicks**, on
Pinktwin. Nowhere else. If you want nanopipes you go to Pinktwin, and there is
no alternative route; it hands over 400 nanopipe quarters, 20 meters and 30
valves in one delivery.

**Nanoframe.** Three sources: Ashbelt Bidirectionals, What Happened To Baobab,
Tungsten Nanocube. Lighter than frame for the same shapes.

**[[part:PowerNanocable]] — the 400 P/s cable.** Three sources: Helirion Lost Package
2, Ascensia Nanopower Cables, Outflowermeter. Until you have one of these, the
[[part:LargeElectricThruster]]'s 160 P/s exceeds what an ordinary [[part:PowerCable]] can
carry, and you own a thruster you cannot properly feed. See
[power and data are two different networks](/guides/wiring-power-and-data).

**Plasma.** Four sources: Aundara Plasma, Wind Test, Tree Lid, Hazard Barrel.

**Big batteries.** Large Disposable comes from five: Moon Large Battery, Kovo
Maneuvers, Ashbelt Bidirectionals, Ascensia Brachiosaurus Egg, Candy Bouquet.
The Moon one is the earliest and the most direct — its entire reward is one
[[part:LargeDisposableBattery]], 100,000 P, which is three times what your eight
starting cells hold put together.

**Solar panels.** Three: Outcast Large Dampers, Unstable Delivery, Triplehead
Sunflower.

## The two objectives that pay best

If you are choosing where to go first, two Package objectives stand out for
sheer breadth of reward:

**CoronaSilva Huge Electro** — fourteen different components. **Earth Lost In
The Snow** and **Baobara Baobab Package** — twelve each. Lost In The Snow is
the one you can do immediately: 150 frames, 25 glass, two Small Electric
Thrusters, two seats, two batteries, 350 data cables, 350 power cables, and the
[[part:Windmeter]], [[part:Aerometer]] and [[part:Atmometer]] — three of the game's environmental
instruments in one package, on your home planet, with no prerequisite.

It is the best opening move in the game, and nothing tells you so.

## Where this comes from

Objective types, dependencies, required components and rewards are read out of
the game's own files and shown on each objective's page in
[Missions](/missions), which will also tell you what a given reward makes
placeable for _your_ save. Nothing here is a walkthrough — this guide is the
shape of the mission graph, not how to complete any objective in it. If a patch
changes the objectives, the mission pages change with it and this guide is what
goes stale.
