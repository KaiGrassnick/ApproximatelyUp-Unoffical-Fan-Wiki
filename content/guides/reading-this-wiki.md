Open this wiki fresh and it looks thin: three places on the galaxy map, a
handful of components, almost no missions. That is deliberate. A wiki that
shows everything is a wiki you cannot open mid-playthrough without learning
what is coming, and most people arrive here in the middle of a game.

So the default is **a new game**. The parts you start with, the places you
start in, nothing else.

There are two ways out of it, and both are yours to choose.

## Drop in your save

The header has a slot for a `.world` file. Drop one in and the wiki opens up
exactly as far as that save has played, and no further — the missions you have
finished, the parts they unlocked, the places you have actually been.

Your saves live here:

```
%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\ApproximatelyGames\ApproximatelyUp\Worlds
```

Each world is one `.world` file. Pick the one you are playing.

**The file never leaves your machine.** It is read by your own browser, and
this site has no server that could receive it — nothing to upload to even if
it wanted to. What the wiki keeps afterwards is a small record of your
progress in your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time from
your browser's site settings. The [privacy policy](/privacy) spells this out.

## Turn the spoiler switches on

If you have finished the game, or you are reading about it rather than playing
it, the save dance is beside the point. The **Spoilers** control above the page
turns things on directly — missions, components, planets and stations, each on
its own switch, or all four at once.

The first switch you turn on asks you to confirm. Only the first, and only
ever once: turning spoilers _off_ again never asks, because hiding something
reveals nothing.

## Which one you want

Loading a save is the better option while you are still playing. It is the only
one that can tell you _what you personally can build right now_, and it will
not get ahead of you.

The switches are the better option once the game holds no more surprises, or if
you just want to browse. They are also the only way to see something a save
cannot reach yet.
